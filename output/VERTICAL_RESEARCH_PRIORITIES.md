- **Why**: High lock-in (patient records, insurance integrations), cloud migration wave, fragmented (dozens of <€10M vendors), no PE consolidation in NL/BE yet.

- **Test companies**: Legisway (BE), Legadex (NL), Soluno (SE — expanding to NL)
- **Proven**: Pipeline scored Clio at B+ with 7 sources in prior test.

### 3. Field Service Management (HVAC/Plumbing) — BENELUX

## Methodology Notes

**What works well with our pipeline:**
- Companies with websites + Wikipedia articles → 5-7 sources, reliable scoring
- Public companies (SEC EDGAR + yfinance) → financial data available
- Benelux companies with GLEIF registration → legal entity confirmed
- Tech companies with GitHub orgs → tech stack analysis, developer community size

**What's hard for our pipeline:**
- Very small companies (no Wikipedia, no GitHub, no GLEIF) → 1-2 sources only
- Non-English websites → tech detection is English-pattern based
- On-premise software vendors → limited web presence
- PE-owned companies with no public info → ownership opacity
- DuckDuckGo HTML search is rate-sensitive (primary bottleneck for batch runs)
