## 4. Ownership & Governance

Booking Experts B.V. (KvK 55796850) is owned by Booking Invest B.V., a Dutch holding company. The founder Ali Ilboga controls the holding. No external PE, VC, or institutional investors have ever held equity. The company also owns Booking Boosters B.V. (marketing agency subsidiary).

The governance structure is clean: a single B.V. under a holding company, with a wholly owned subsidiary for marketing services. No complex cap table, no PE overhang, no liquidation preferences. The founder's age is estimated at ~45 (based on founding timeline) — not an immediate succession risk but a consideration for long-term planning.

The company acquired/invested in ParcVu (UK) in September 2024 — an outbound strategic investment that signals Ali Ilboga is still in building mode, not actively seeking an exit.

## 7. Overall Assessment

Booking Experts is the strongest M&A candidate in the entire HORECA universe. It is clean (no PE, no debt), profitable (15+ years bootstrapped), technically excellent (cloud-native, OpenAPI), and defensible (market leader with high switching costs). The composite score of 4.59 reflects near-perfect alignment with SolStein's Benelux thesis.

Primary risks: (1) the founder may not want to sell — the ParcVu outbound investment signals building mode; (2) at an estimated €8-12M ARR, valuation expectations could be €25-50M+ (3-5× ARR), exceeding SolStein's typical range; (3) family management (Maureen Ilboga as COO) could mean the business is a family asset with no exit intent; (4) revenue is estimated with significant uncertainty due to no public financials.

**Tier**: Tier 1 / Primary Target
