## 4. Ownership & Governance

TableFever is entirely founder-owned with no external investors. Stef van der Schueren founded the company in 2014 and has operated it independently ever since. He also owns PITTIG.be (a separate hospitality-related business).

The founder publicly declined a 2022 acquisition offer from Zenchef, stating: "We are convinced there is still room for national players who offer tailored service, which large players like Zenchef cannot provide." This signals both strong founder conviction and a clear understanding of the competitive dynamic.

The 4.6 FTE team is extremely lean for a company managing 2,000 restaurant accounts. This is both a risk (key-man dependency on the founder) and an opportunity (clean deal structure, low integration complexity).

## 7. Overall Assessment

TableFever is a high-value micro-cap acquisition target for SolStein's Benelux platform. It offers:
- **Strategic monopoly**: Last independent BE reservation player after Zenchef consolidation
- **Exceptional financials**: 52% net margin, 30%+ gross margin growth, extremely capital efficient
- **Defensive value**: Denies PSG/Zenchef the Belgian completeness they want
- **Cross-sell surface**: Every TableFever restaurant is a potential unTill POS and Apicbase kitchen lead

Primary risks: (1) The €4-8M estimated acquisition EV may become €15M+ if multiple bidders (Zenchef, Access Group) compete; (2) The Zenchef tailwind (churn absorption from Resengo/Tablebooker) decays over 24-36 months; (3) Service-heavy model limits pure SaaS scalability; (4) Key-man dependency on the founder.

**Tier**: Tier 1 / Primary Acquisition Target
