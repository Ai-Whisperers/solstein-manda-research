## 4. Ownership & Governance

unTill Software Development Group B.V. (KvK 63559706) is a privately held Dutch B.V. The principal has been André Kortlever since 2004. No PE or VC involvement — the cap table is clean.

However, the **reseller structure is the dominant governance concern**. The development entity employs 6 people and licenses the software through 15 independent reseller companies (3 NL: UPTA, Adnamics, Janssens; 12 BE: BNCX, XPOS, AlfaPOS Control, Kassa-Systemen.be, Rakedi, Rubbens & Co, Sekuriet, CuComp Technics, BC-Matic, Microvolt, VDS Solutions, Active Office). Each is a separate legal entity with its own customer relationships, support contracts, and local market knowledge.

This creates a **double-edged acquisition path**:
- **Feature**: Acquiring the IP entity transfers the software and brand. The resellers can be absorbed or retained.
- **Risk**: The resellers hold the customer relationships. If they don't transfer loyalty post-acquisition, the 8,000+ customer base could erode. 12 BE resellers with separate ownership structures is significant deal complexity.

No external investors, no PE. The founder's exit posture is unknown but the 2024 product launches (Air, Payments) and international reseller expansion (15+ countries) are consistent with asset-polishing ahead of a sale.

## 7. Overall Assessment

unTill is a moderately attractive M&A candidate for SolStein's Benelux HORECA platform. The company has:
- **Dominant NL market position**: Market leader with 3,500+ domestic customers
- **Technical bridge**: Open API with 125+ integrations; Air product (2024) begins cloud migration
- **Cross-sell surface**: Every unTill customer is a potential TableFever and Apicbase lead
- **Growth momentum**: New products (Air, Payments) + international reseller expansion (15+ countries)

The primary concerns are: (1) fragmented dev+reseller structure (15 entities) creates significant deal complexity — the IP sits separately from customer relationships; (2) financials are entirely opaque — no filed accounts, no confirmed revenue, no profitability data; (3) founder identity and exit posture are unconfirmed; (4) PHP/ASP.NET legacy stack is a modernization liability. The headcount decline at the NL dev entity (8→6, -21.4% YoY) is a cautionary signal but may reflect NL-specific consolidation while international resellers grow.

**Tier**: Watch (needs founder-contact and at least 3 years of filed accounts before classification can be confirmed)
