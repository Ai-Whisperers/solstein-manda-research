## Executive Summary

The Ai-Whisperers org has already done **massive Paraguay market research** — more than most PE firms have. The leads repo has:
- **7,463 businesses** scraped from Google Maps (beauty/wellness vertical)
- **144 rubros** (business categories) mapped to builder verticals
- **209 cities** covered nationwide
- **75% have no website** — 5,602 potential clients
- **3,960 Priority A leads** (quality score 85-100)
- **110 demo sites** planned across all rubro categories

What the Solstein M&A pipeline could add: **systematic enrichment** of these leads with company data, funding, revenue estimates, and tech stack analysis — turning raw lead lists into investable deal flow.

1. **Add Google Maps API** as a source for the pipeline — currently the leads repo has lat/lng, ratings, and phone data for 7,463 businesses that the pipeline doesn't know about
2. **Run pipeline on top 50 Paraguay leads** to find which have the strongest digital signals (SerpAPI KG, GitHub, funding data)
3. **Score Paraguay companies** against Solstein's 8 dimensions — the Geographic fit dimension would need a new "Paraguay thesis" weight

1. The pipeline's **SerpAPI** source can find companies without websites (knowledge graph exists but no domain resolves)
2. **AbstractAPI** can classify businesses by industry for better lead routing
3. **LinkedIn employee estimates** can prioritize leads by company size
